1 · Bring someone to the exchange

Live now

Introduce someone who buys PCN

Share your personal link. When the person who follows it opens an account on exchange.pc.am, funds it and buys PCN, you are paid.

200 PCN
per person
  1. Sign in at exchange.pc.am. You sign in with your PCoin account — the same one you use at market.pc.am. If you do not have one, creating it is part of signing in.
  2. Copy your personal link. It looks like exchange.pc.am/?ref=XXXXXXXX and it is on your account page. The code is yours and does not change.
  3. Share it. Anywhere you like, to people who would genuinely want it.
  4. They open your link and create an account. You are credited with the introduction the moment they first click it, not when they sign up — so it still counts if they come back a week later. If they had already visited through someone else's link, that first link keeps them.
  5. They deposit at least $50 and buy PCN with it. Both are required. A deposit with no purchase does not count, and neither does buying with PCN they already had.
  6. Wait 14 days. Everything is re-checked at the end of that period.
  7. 200 PCN appears in your exchange balance. You can spend it on the exchange straight away, or withdraw it once your balance is over the $30 withdrawal minimum.
The rules
  • One payment per person you introduce. Introducing the same person twice pays once.
  • You cannot introduce yourself, and accounts sharing a payment method are checked by a person before anything is paid.
  • The pool is 3,000 PCN — fifteen payments. When it is gone the programme stops until it is topped up. It is paid in the order people qualify.

2 · Run a mining pool that is not ours

Live now

Run an independent pool, get 5 miners

Almost all PCoin mining goes through one pool, and that pool is ours. That is not healthy for the network and we would rather pay to fix it than pretend otherwise.

500 PCN
per pool
  1. Run a PCoin mining pool. Any pool software that speaks Stratum and can build PCoin blocks. If you would rather not write one, use ours — it is the same software we run in production, in the public repository under the same licence, with nothing held back: Running a PCoin pool. You host it, you run it, you keep the fees you charge.
  2. Attract at least 5 miners. Five separate payout addresses. That is what the chain shows and what we count — we cannot tell a person from a machine and we do not pretend to.
  3. Be active across the month. We look at the last 30 days and need to have seen your pool finding blocks on at least 7 separate days in that window. One lucky block paying six addresses is not five miners; you do not have to have been running for the whole month.
  4. Tell us it exists — in the PCoin chat or by opening an issue on GitHub. Say where the pool is and where you would like to be paid.
  5. We check it on the blockchain. A pool pays its miners inside the block it finds, so we simply count how many separate addresses your pool has been paying, over those 30 days. We do not need access to your server, your miner list, or anything else you would reasonably not want to give us.
  6. We pay you 500 PCN to the address you gave us. Miners reach you with SRBMiner-Multi (--algorithm randompcn --pool your.host:3333), which needs nothing from us and no special build — re-checked against a live PCoin pool on 17 September 2026.
The rules
  • Paid once per pool.
  • Your pool must be genuinely independent — a pool we do not run, and not a relay pointing at ours. Running our pool software is fine and encouraged; it is running our instance that is not a second pool.
  • We tell you what we counted. If we say no, you get the number we saw and why.

What is not on this list, and why

We do not pay for app store reviews, ratings or installs — Google prohibits it and it would put the app at risk. We do not pay for posts on Reddit, which prohibits incentivised posting. And we do not pay for followers, group members, stars or likes. Those are the easiest things in the world to buy, and buying them would tell us nothing true about PCoin and tell you even less.

What you are being paid in

PCN is the coin of an independent blockchain that is about six weeks old. Please be clear about what that means before taking part:

  • PCN is not listed on any exchange other than our own.
  • What PCN does do today is pay for real things — the services listed above accept it, and you can mine it yourself on an ordinary computer.
  • We publish a rate at price.pc.am. That is the rate our own services credit a payment at. It is not a market price, and it is not a promise that anyone will buy your PCN at it.

We would rather say that plainly and have fewer people take part than have anyone join expecting something we have not got.
